My College Experience

As I became a college student, I never expected adulthood to involve so many real-life experiences and responsibilities similar to those our parents face. I was enlightened by how college teaches us about maturity and personal growth.

I face challenges such as arriving late to class, struggling to understand some topics, and trying to catch up on lessons I missed.

Overall, I believe my journey into adulthood begins here, not simply when I turn 18. I will do my best to get through the challenges of academic life and reach eudaimonia.

Primary Reflection

My Personality Outside School

When I'm not at school or outside alone, I act enthusiastic and often become a silly billy. I'm also playful and like to mix things with comedy and humor.

I learned that my true self comes out when I feel comfortable, especially when I'm alone or around my parents and relatives.

To sum it up, I'm an ambivert, which means I'm somewhere between an introvert and an extrovert.
